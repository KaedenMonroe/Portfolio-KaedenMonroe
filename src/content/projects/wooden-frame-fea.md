For this Mechanics of Solids project, I designed a wooden frame assembly and evaluated its structural performance under load - starting with manual hand calculations for shear and deflection to establish a target safety factor, then validating those results against Finite Element Analysis.

The FEA pass served two purposes: confirming the manual calculations held up under a more rigorous load model, and identifying opportunities to optimize material usage without compromising structural integrity. I carried the validated design through to fabrication-ready documentation — comprehensive 2D technical drawings and a full Bill of Materials, built to GD&T principles.

This project was as much an exercise in cross-checking analytical methods against simulation as it was in the design itself: the manual and FEA results had to agree before the design was considered validated.
