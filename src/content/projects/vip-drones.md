As Sector Lead for the Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) Drones program at UD, I direct sector-level operations spanning software, hardware, and field data collection — managing budget, critical path scheduling, and a drone fleet used for autonomous site analysis.

On the software side, I developed automated Python pipelines to convert raw 2D UAV video into high-fidelity 3D environments using Gaussian Splatting and photogrammetry. To keep reconstruction viable on constrained hardware, I integrated active frame pruning during capture and post-generation Gaussian pruning, cutting memory usage enough to support real-time rendering on mobile platforms. The resulting 3D site reconstructions feed directly into BIM integration and site design workflows.

On the operations side, I manage the Bill of Materials and vendor relationships (including DJI) for hardware repairs, and lead electro-mechanical maintenance on the fleet - diagnosing and repairing internal component failures with multimeters and soldering equipment. I also conduct autonomous and manual aerial data collection missions, applying sensor fusion techniques to support volume reconstruction and object tracking.

To keep the sector coordinated as it scaled, I set up and now manage our internal team platform: Notion for documentation, SOPs, and knowledge management, Linear for task tracking and sprint-style planning across the software and hardware sub-teams. This replaced ad hoc messaging-based coordination and gave the sector a persistent record of decisions, open issues, and onboarding material for new members.

I authored the team's SOPs for program deployment to standardize process consistency and scalability as the sector has grown.
